## 2. Self-introduction (60-90 seconds)

### Structure

| Step | Content |
|---|---|
| 1 | Name, university, degree, why this major |
| 2 | Experience: thesis, tutoring, two jobs |
| 3 | Goal after the program |
| 4 | Why HCMUS |

### Script (replace [Name])

> Good morning. My name is [Name]. I graduated from the University of Science, VNU-HCM, in 2019, with a degree in Nuclear Engineering from the Faculty of Physics and Engineering Physics.
>
> I have loved physics since high school. For my thesis, I developed a procedure to treat radium contamination in soil and water, using the BCR and MnO2 methods. This work taught me how to do experiments, analyze data, and present results.
>
> I have also worked as a physics tutor, helping high school students prepare for the national exam. After graduation, I worked as a product quality inspector at Bureau Veritas Vietnam from September 2020 to November 2022. Since 2022, I have worked as a quality supervisor at MPE Electric Company. These jobs made me careful, accurate, and responsible.
>
> Through teaching and working, I realized that I want to become a high school physics teacher. So I chose the master's program in Engineering Physics, in experimental physics teaching. I want to improve my knowledge and learn STEM teaching methods, so that students can learn physics in a practical and easy way.
>
> I chose this university because it is strong in physics, and I already know the teachers and the learning environment here. Thank you.

## 3. Guided conversation (15 points)

### Answer formula: A.R.E.F (30-45 seconds)

| Letter | Function | Sample phrase |
|---|---|---|
| **A**nswer | Direct answer | "I want to..." / "I chose it because..." |
| **R**eason | Why | "The main reason is that..." |
| **E**xample | Personal case | "For example, when I tutored..." |
| **F**uture | Closing | "So in the future, I hope to..." |

### Core Q&A bank

| Question | Model answer |
|---|---|
| Why did you choose this major? | I have loved physics since high school. While working, I realized I want to teach. This major fits my background, my interest, and my career goal. For example, tutoring showed me how much I enjoy explaining physics. |
| What are your goals after the program? | I want to become a high school physics teacher. I hope to learn experimental teaching methods and STEM education, so my students can connect physics with real life. |
| Why did you choose this university? | It has a good reputation in physics. I graduated here, so I know the environment and the teachers. I believe it is the best place for me to continue learning. |
| Tell me about your experience. | My thesis was about radium contamination in soil and water. I also tutored physics, and I worked in quality inspection for about five years. These taught me research skills, teaching skills, and accuracy. |

### Likely follow-up questions

| Question | Model answer |
|---|---|
| Why change from quality control to teaching? | In my work I enjoyed training colleagues and explaining technical points. Together with tutoring, this made me realize teaching is my real passion. |
| What is STEM education? | It combines Science, Technology, Engineering, and Math. Students learn by doing experiments and solving real problems. |
| How will you use experiments in class? | I will use simple experiments, such as measuring motion or building electric circuits, so students can see the theory work. |
| What was difficult in your thesis? | Designing the procedure and getting accurate data. I learned to be patient and to check every step. |
| What did your supervisor teach you? | He inspired my passion for experimental physics and showed me how to do research carefully. |
| How does your work help your teaching? | Quality inspection taught me to follow procedures and be precise, which is important in lab work. |
| What makes a good physics teacher? | Clear explanations, patience, and practical examples. |
| What challenges do students face in physics? | Many students find it abstract. Experiments make it concrete and interesting. |

## 4. Useful phrases

### Fillers (never stay silent)
- "Well..."
- "Let me think..."
- "That's an interesting question."

### Asking for repetition (no penalty)
- "Sorry, could you repeat that, please?"
- "Could you say that more slowly, please?"

### Missing a word
- "It is the thing that we use to..."
- "I mean, a kind of..."

### Linking words
firstly, also, in addition, however, for example, as a result, so, because

## 6. Tactics on test day

1. Use short sentences: present simple, past simple, present perfect.
2. Fewer errors beat ambitious grammar.
3. Steer answers back to strengths: physics, experiments, teaching.
4. Speak at a steady pace, with eye contact.
5. Aim for 3-5 sentences per answer.
