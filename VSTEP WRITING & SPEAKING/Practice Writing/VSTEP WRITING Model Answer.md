TASK 1-EMAIL  
  
You have recently decided to keep a pet, and you are interested in buying one from a local pet shop.  
  
Write an email to the pet shop. In your email, you should:  
  
- say what kind of pet you are interested in;  
- ask about the pet's age, health and characteristics;  
- ask about the price and what food or equipment the pet needs;  
- ask whether the shop provides any advice or support after purchase.  
  
Write at least 120 words.  
  
Dear Sir or Madam,  
  
I am writing to ask for some information about the pets available at your shop. I am currently looking for a suitable pet to keep at home, and I am particularly interested in having a small dog.  
  
First, I would like to know how old the dogs are and whether they have received the necessary vaccinations. I would also appreciate it if you could tell me about their health and personality, as I am looking for a friendly and easy-to-care-for pet.  
  
In addition, could you please let me know how much the dog costs? I would also like to know what kind of food, toys and other equipment I need to prepare before bringing it home.  
  
Finally, I would be grateful if you could tell me whether your shop provides any advice or support after purchase.  
  
I look forward to hearing from you soon.  
  
Yours faithfully, Thanh Ha  
  
Word count: 136 words

TASK 2- ESSAY  
  
Being famous can bring many benefits, but it can also cause some problems. Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of being famous.  
  
Write at least 250 words.  
  
Nowadays, being famous is a dream for many people, especially young people. Although fame can bring several benefits, it can also create some problems. This essay will discuss both the advantages and disadvantages of being famous.  
  
On the one hand, being famous can bring a number of benefits. First, famous people often have better career opportunities and can earn a large amount of money. For example, successful singers, actors and athletes can receive high salaries and many valuable contracts. Second, fame gives people a strong public influence. They can use their popularity to promote useful messages, support charities and encourage people to do good things. Therefore, becoming famous can provide both financial benefits and opportunities to make a positive impact on society.  
  
On the other hand, fame also has some disadvantages. The first problem is the lack of privacy. Famous people are often followed by the media and may find it difficult to enjoy a normal private life. In addition, they may face constant public attention and pressure. Every mistake they make can quickly become a topic of discussion on social media. This can make their lives stressful and prevent them from feeling relaxed. Another disadvantage is that some famous people may have difficulty knowing who their true friends are, because others may approach them because of their money or popularity.  
  
In conclusion, being famous has both advantages and  
  
disadvantages. While it can provide better career opportunities, financial benefits and the chance to influence society, it can also lead to a loss of privacy and considerable pressure. Therefore, people who become famous need to learn how to deal with public attention and maintain a balanced life.  
  
Word count: 287 words
