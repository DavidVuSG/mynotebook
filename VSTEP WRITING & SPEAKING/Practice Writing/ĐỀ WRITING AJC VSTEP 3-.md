## Task 2 – Essay


Nowadays, cashless payment methods such as credit cards, mobile apps, and e-wallets have become increasingly popular.

**What are the advantages and disadvantages of a cashless society?**

  **Give reasons and examples to support your answer. Write about 250 words.**

  

### Sample Answer:

  

In recent years, digital transactions have become more common, moving many countries toward a cashless society (xã hội không dùng tiền mặt). While this trend brings significant convenience, it also has certain drawbacks.

  On the one hand, paying without cash offers several clear benefits. The most obvious advantage is convenience and speed (sự tiện lợi và nhanh chóng). Instead of carrying bulky wallets or waiting for change, people can pay bills instantly by tapping a card or scanning a QR code with their smartphones. In addition, cashless transactions improve personal safety (tăng cường an toàn cá nhân) because people do not have to carry large sums of money, which reduces the risk of pickpocketing or theft. Finally, digital records help users track their spending history easily through banking apps.
  
On the other hand, there are notable disadvantages to consider. A major concern is cybercrime and security risks (tội phạm mạng và rủi ro bảo mật). Digital systems are vulnerable to hackers, phishing scams, and technical glitches, which can lead to identity theft or financial loss. Another negative effect is that cashless methods may cause impulsive buying (mua sắm bốc đồng). When individuals do not physically hand over cash, they often lose track of how much they spend, leading to unexpected debts. Lastly, elderly citizens and people in rural areas who lack digital skills may struggle to adapt to this modern payment system.

  In conclusion, although a cashless society brings great speed and safety, it also poses challenges related to cyber fraud and financial self-discipline (tính kỷ luật tài chính). People should learn to manage their budgets carefully and protect their personal information online.
