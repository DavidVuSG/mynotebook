#VSTEP #writing 

> **Hedging** is used to make your arguments less absolute and more academic.  
> Instead of saying something is **100% true**, we show that it is **possible, likely, or generally true**.

---

## 1. Weaker Verbs

### Structure

```text
S + seems / appears + to + V
```

### Useful patterns

```text
S seems to + V
S appears to + V
It seems that + S + V
It appears that + S + V
```

### Examples

> Planting trees **seems to reduce** air pollution.

> Online learning **appears to provide** students with greater flexibility.

> It **seems that** young people are becoming more dependent on technology.

### ⭐ VSTEP template

```text
[Subject] seems/appears to + V + ...
```

---

## 2. Modal Verbs

### Structure

```text
S + can / could / may / might + V
```

### Examples

> Planting trees **could help reduce** air pollution.

> Online learning **may improve** access to education.

> Working from home **might increase** employees' productivity.

> Public transport **can reduce** traffic congestion.

### ⭐ VSTEP template

```text
[Subject] could/may/might + V + ...
```

### 💡 When to use

Use these when you want to say that something is **possible**, rather than stating it as an absolute fact.

```text
could → may → might
```

All three are useful for hedging.

---

# 3. Adverbs

## A. Adverbs of Certainty

### Useful words

```text
possibly
probably
certainly
evidently
```

### Structure

```text
Adverb + V
```

or

```text
be + adverb + adjective/noun
```

### Examples

> This policy **will probably reduce** unemployment.

> This approach is **possibly an effective** way to solve the problem.

> Technology **certainly plays** an important role in modern education.

### ⭐ VSTEP template

```text
S + probably/possibly + V + ...
```

---

## B. Adverbs of Frequency

### Useful words

```text
usually
often
sometimes
generally
```

These are useful because they avoid making **absolute generalisations**.

### Example

❌ Too strong:

> Young people **always** use social media.

✅ Better:

> Young people **often** use social media.

✅ More academic:

> Young people **generally** use social media to communicate with others.

### ⭐ Remember

Avoid overusing:

```text
always
never
everyone
nobody
all
completely
```

Use:

```text
usually
often
sometimes
generally
```

---

# 4. Nouns Expressing Certainty / Possibility

## Structure

```text
There + be + a/an + noun + that + S + V
```

### Useful nouns

```text
certainty
possibility
probability
tendency
evidence
```

### Examples

> There is **a possibility that** this policy will increase unemployment.

> There is **a probability that** house prices will continue to rise.

> There is **evidence that** regular exercise improves people's health.

---

## ⭐ Tendency

A more natural structure is:

```text
There is a tendency for + N + to + V
```

### Example

> There is **a tendency for young people to spend** more time online.

Another useful structure:

```text
S + tends to + V
```

> Young people **tend to spend** more time on social media.

---

# 5. Strong → Hedged

This is the most important idea to remember.

### Strong

> Technology **improves** education.

### Hedged

> Technology **can improve** education.

### More cautious

> Technology **could improve** education.

### Even more cautious

> Technology **might improve** education.

---

### Another example

**Strong:**

> Social media **causes** mental health problems.

**Hedged:**

> Excessive use of social media **may cause** mental health problems.

**More academic:**

> Excessive use of social media **seems to contribute to** mental health problems.

> [!TIP] ⭐ Best ones to memorise for VSTEP  
> You **do not need to use all of them**. For a B2 essay, these 6 are enough:
> 
> 1. `S seems to + V`
>     
> 2. `S appears to + V`
>     
> 3. `S could + V`
>     
> 4. `S may + V`
>     
> 5. `There is a possibility that + S + V`
>     
> 6. `There is a tendency for + N + to + V`
>

### Example paragraph using hedging

> **Online learning seems to offer** several advantages for students. It **can provide** greater flexibility and **may reduce** educational costs. However, there is **a possibility that** some students might feel isolated because they have fewer opportunities to interact with their classmates. Therefore, online learning **could be** an effective option, but it may not be suitable for everyone.

This is the kind of **natural hedging** you want in VSTEP Task 2—not forcing a hedging structure into every sentence.
