# 📚 VSTEP B1 - Speaking & Writing Master Guide & AI Templates

Tài liệu tra cứu cấu trúc, khung câu trả lời mẫu (Template) và tập dữ liệu câu hỏi thực hành cho bài thi **VSTEP B1 (Speaking & Writing)**. Được tối ưu hóa cho **Obsidian Markdown** và làm **Context Reference / Prompt Template** cho các mô hình AI.

---

## 🗣️ A. SPEAKING SKILLS

### 📌 Part 1: Social Interaction (3 Minutes)

#### 1. Tổng quan & Dạng câu hỏi
- **Đặc điểm**: Đề gồm 2 chủ đề lớn, mỗi chủ đề 3 câu hỏi liên quan đến bản thân, gia đình, công việc, sở thích.
- **Mục tiêu**: Làm quen, dẫn dắt và phản xạ nhanh.

#### 2. Chiến lược trả lời theo từ hỏi (AI / Human Pattern)

| Từ hỏi                                    | Nghĩa                   | Công thức phản hồi (Template Structure)                       | Ví dụ minh họa                                                                                                |
| :---------------------------------------- | :---------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Yes/No** (`Do you...?` / `Can you...?`) | Bạn có... không?        | `[Yes/No] + [S + V] + because it is + [Adj]`                  | *Do you like shopping?*<br>→ Yes, I do because it is interesting and relaxing.                                |
| **WHO**                                   | Ai                      | `My favorite [person] is [Name/Relation]`                     | *Who is your favorite singer?*<br>→ My favorite singer is Son Tung M-TP.                                      |
| **WHAT**                                  | Cái gì / Làm gì         | `I [action / object] for / in [context]`                      | *What do you do in your free time?*<br>→ I read books and watch TV in my free time.                           |
| **WHICH**                                 | Lựa chọn                | `I prefer [Option A] because it is [Adj]`                     | *Which do you prefer A or B?*<br>→ I prefer A because it is better.                                           |
| **WHEN**                                  | Khi nào                 | `I often [action] in the [time] / when [clause]`              | *When do you often play sports?*<br>→ I often play sports in the morning because it makes me healthy.         |
| **WHERE**                                 | Ở đâu                   | `I often [action] at/in [Place]`                              | *Where do you often spend your free time?*<br>→ I often spend my free time at home because it is comfortable. |
| **HOW**                                   | Bằng cách nào / Thế nào | `I [action] by [V-ing]` <br> `I feel [Emotion] about [Topic]` | *How do you learn new words?*<br>→ I learn new words by watching movies with subtitles.                       |
| **HOW OFTEN**                             | Tần suất                | `I [action] [Frequency] because [Reason]`                     | *How often do you go to the cinema?*<br>→ I go to the cinema once a week because I love movies.               |
| **HOW LONG**                              | Thời gian bao lâu       | `I [action] in [Time Period] because [Reason]`                | *How long will you finish the project?*<br>→ I will finish the project in one week because it is easy.        |

#### 3. AI Master Template - Speaking Part 1
> [!NOTE] 
> Sử dụng khung này để AI tự động điền các thành phần trả lời bài nói Part 1.

```text
Let's talk about [Topic].
- First of all, regarding [Rephrase Question 1], [Answer 1] because [Reason 1].
- Second of all, as for [Rephrase Question 2], [Answer 2] because [Reason 2].
- Third of all, concerning [Rephrase Question 3], [Answer 3] because [Reason 3].
```

#### 4. Ví dụ mẫu (Sample Output)
- **Topic**: Family & Friends
  - **Question 1**: How many people are there in your family?
  - **Question 2**: Who do you spend your free time with?
  - **Question 3**: Who are the most important people in your life?

```text
Let's talk about Family and Friends.
- First of all, regarding how many people there are in my family, there are 4 people in my family: my wife, my children, and me, and we are very happy together.
- Second of all, as for who I spend my free time with, I usually spend my free time with my son. We often play chess or football together because it is very fun.
- Third of all, concerning who the most important people in my life are, the most important person in my life is my wife because she is very caring and supportive.
```

### 📌 Part 2: Solution Discussion (4 Minutes)

#### 1. Tổng quan
- Cho 1 tình huống đi kèm **3 lựa chọn**.
- **Yêu cầu**: Chọn 1 giải pháp tốt nhất, giải thích lý do chọn và lý do loại bỏ 2 phương án còn lại.

#### 2. AI Master Template - Speaking Part 2

```text
[Rephrase Situation from Prompt].
There are three choices given: [Option A], [Option B], and [Option C].
In my opinion, I will choose [Selected Option] for several reasons.

- First of all, it is [Reason 1 / Advantage 1].
- Second of all, it [Reason 2 / Advantage 2].

On the other hand, I don't choose [Rejected Option 1] because it is [Disadvantage 1].
Similarly, I don't prefer [Rejected Option 2] because it is [Disadvantage 2].

To sum up, I strongly believe that [Selected Option] is the best choice for this situation.
```

#### 3. Ví dụ mẫu (Sample Output)
- **Situation**: One of your friends is planning for a holiday with his friends. He asked you for some advice about where to go: Sapa, Nha Trang, or Singapore. What do you think is the best choice? Why?

```text
One of my friends is planning a holiday with his friends and he asked me for advice about where to go: Sapa, Nha Trang, or Singapore. 
There are three choices available: going to Sapa, Nha Trang, or Singapore. 
In my opinion, I will choose Nha Trang for several reasons. 
- First of all, it is very beautiful with many famous beaches. 
- Second of all, traveling to Nha Trang is quite cheap and suitable for students.

On the other hand, I don't choose Sapa because it is very far and tiring to travel. 
Similarly, I don't prefer Singapore because it is extremely expensive.

To sum up, I strongly believe that visiting Nha Trang is the best choice for them.
```

### 📌 Part 3: Topic Development (5 Minutes)

#### 1. Tổng quan
- Bài nói phát triển theo **Sơ đồ tư duy (Mind Map)** gồm 1 Chủ đề chính + 3 Ý gợi ý.
- Cuối phần thi có 3 câu hỏi phụ (Follow-up Questions).

#### 2. AI Master Template - Speaking Part 3

```text
Well, I would like to talk about the topic: "[Main Topic Statement]".

- First of all, the first point I want to mention is [Point 1]. [Explanation / Example 1].
- Second of all, the second point is [Point 2]. [Explanation / Example 2].
- Third of all, the final point is [Point 3]. [Explanation / Example 3].

To sum up, [Rephrase Main Topic Statement].
```

#### 3. Ví dụ mẫu (Sample Output)
- **Topic**: Smoking should be banned in public places.
  - Point 1: Smoker's health
  - Point 2: Second-hand smoker's health
  - Point 3: Financial burden

```text
Well, I would like to talk about the topic: "Smoking should be banned in public places."

- First of all, the first point I want to mention is smoker's health. Smoking causes severe diseases like lung cancer, so it is dangerous for smokers themselves.
- Second of all, the second point is second-hand smoker's health. Non-smokers who inhale passive smoke can suffer from health problems as well.
- Third of all, the final point is financial burden. Buying cigarettes continuously costs a lot of money that could be spent on better things.

To sum up, I strongly agree that smoking should be banned in public places.
```

## ✍️ B. WRITING SKILLS

### ✉️ Part 1: Letter/Email Writing (120+ Words - 20 Minutes)

#### 1. AI Master Template - Informal / Semi-Formal Email

```text
Dear [Recipient Name],

I'm so happy to receive your email. How are your family and your work? Please send my warm regards to your parents.
I am writing this email to answer the questions you asked in your last email.

- First of all, regarding [Question 1 Topic], [Answer 1 + Supporting Detail].
- Second of all, as for [Question 2 Topic], [Answer 2 + Supporting Detail].
- Third of all, concerning [Question 3 Topic], [Answer 3 + Supporting Detail].
- Finally, about [Question 4 Topic (if any)], [Answer 4 + Supporting Detail].

I hope this information helps!
Looking forward to seeing you soon.

Best wishes,
[Sender Name]
```

#### 2. Ví dụ mẫu (Sample Output)
- **Prompt**: An English friend (Kate) asks about your friend An, who wants to stay with her family in London.

```text
Dear Kate,

I'm so happy to receive your email. How are your family and your work? Please send my warm regards to your parents.
I am writing this email to answer the questions you asked in your last email about my friend, An.

First of all, speaking of what she is like, An is extremely intelligent, friendly, and hard-working. She is currently one of the top students in my class. 

Second of all, regarding her hobbies and interests, she loves reading books and cooking. She frequently cooks delicious meals for her family and friends, so I am sure she will make great food for yours too.

Finally, concerning her current study, she is studying Economics at Hanoi University. She is very responsible, so I am confident that she will fit in with your family perfectly.

I hope this information helps!
Looking forward to seeing you soon.

Best wishes,
[Your Name]
```

### 📝 Part 2: Essay Writing (250+ Words - 40 Minutes)

#### 🌐 Dạng 1: Advantages & Disadvantages Essay

##### AI Master Template:
```text
[Topic Statement / Paraphrase Context]. In recent years, this issue has become a major concern among the public. In this essay, I will discuss both the advantages and disadvantages of [Topic].

On the one hand, there are several distinct benefits associated with [Topic]. First of all, [Advantage 1 + Explanation/Example]. Second of all, [Advantage 2 + Explanation/Example].

On the other hand, certain drawbacks cannot be ignored. First of all, [Disadvantage 1 + Explanation/Example]. Second of all, [Disadvantage 2 + Explanation/Example].

To sum up, every coin has two sides. While there are some negative aspects, the positive impacts of [Topic] are also significant. In my personal opinion, I believe that [State Personal View].
```

---

#### ⚖️ Dạng 2: Agree or Disagree Essay

##### AI Master Template:
```text
[Topic Statement / Paraphrase Context]. This statement has sparked a lot of debate in society. In this essay, I will share my opinions on whether [Rephrase Target Statement].

On the one hand, there are valid reasons to support this view. First of all, [Supporting Argument 1 + Explanation]. Second of all, [Supporting Argument 2 + Explanation].

On the other hand, opponents of this idea present strong counterarguments. First of all, [Opposing Argument 1 + Explanation]. Second of all, [Opposing Argument 2 + Explanation].

In conclusion, every issue has two sides. After examining both perspectives, I strongly [Agree / Disagree] with the idea that [Rephrase Target Statement].
```

## 🗂️ C. PRACTICE QUESTION BANK (NGÂN HÀNG ĐỀ THI)

> [!TIP]
> Sử dụng danh sách dưới đây làm đầu vào (Input Prompt) để yêu cầu AI sinh bài mẫu chuẩn VSTEP.

### 🗣️ Speaking Practice Sets

#### Set 1: Social Interaction
1. **Shopping**: Where do you usually go shopping? | Who do you like going shopping with? | Do you prefer going shopping alone or with friends?
2. **Sports**: What is your favorite sport? | Which sports would you like to learn? Why? | Why do people play sports?
3. **Transport**: How do you go to work? | How long does it take you to get to work? | Is it easy to travel in your city?
4. **House & Home**: How many rooms are there in your house? | What is your favorite room in the house? | What do you want to change in your house?
5. **Weather**: What's the weather like in your place in different seasons? | What kind of weather do you enjoy? | Which month is the hottest/coldest?
6. **Neighborhood**: Can you tell me something about your neighborhood? | What do you like most about it? | Do you plan to live there for a long time? Why/why not?

2. **Pet Selection**: Giving a pet to grandparents in the countryside: a dog, a cat, or a fish. Which is most suitable?
3. **Lottery Spending**: A friend won 1,000,000,000 VNĐ. Options: buy a house, buy a car, or save in the bank. Choose the best idea.

#### Set 3: Topic Development
1. **Online Courses**:
   - Gợi ý: Provide new learning experiences | Be more convenient to study | Be independent in learning | Your own idea.

2. **Environmental Protection**:
   - Gợi ý: Use alternative energy | Use public transport | Recycle | Your own idea.
   - Questions: What can you recycle? | Have you done enough to protect the environment? | Should schools teach environmental protection?
