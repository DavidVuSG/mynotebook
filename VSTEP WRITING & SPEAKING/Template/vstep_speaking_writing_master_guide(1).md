# 📚 VSTEP Speaking & Writing Master Guide & AI Templates

Tài liệu tra cứu toàn diện cấu trúc, khung câu trả lời mẫu (Template), quy tắc mở rộng ý và tập dữ liệu thực hành cho bài thi **VSTEP (Speaking & Writing)**. 
Tài liệu này được thiết kế tối ưu hóa cho **Obsidian Markdown** và dùng làm **Context Reference / Prompt Template** cho các mô hình AI reference khi tạo phản hồi.

## 🗣️ A. SPEAKING SKILLS

---

### 📌 Part 1: Social Interaction (3 Minutes)

#### 1. Tổng quan & Quy tắc cốt lõi
- **Thời lượng**: ~3 phút (2 chủ đề lớn, mỗi chủ đề 3 câu hỏi).
- **Mục tiêu**: Phản xạ nhanh, tự nhiên, trả lời đúng trọng tâm.
- **Thời lượng mỗi câu**: 3–5 câu (20–30 giây). **Không bao giờ trả lời 1 từ hoặc 1 câu đơn**.

---

#### 2. Công thức Master: A.R.E.F
Mọi câu hỏi Part 1 đều có thể mở rộng theo công thức **A.R.E.F**:

| Bước                   | Chức năng                   | Từ/Cụm từ bắt đầu                                           |
| :--------------------- | :-------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------- |
| **A** (Answer)         | Trả lời trực tiếp câu hỏi   | *Yes, I really do. / I tend to... / My favourite... is...*  |
| **R** (Reason)         | Đưa ra lý do giải thích     | *...because... / ...since... / The main reason is that...*  |
| **E** (Example)        | Đưa ra ví dụ cụ thể         | *For instance,... / For example,... / Last week I...*       |
| **F** (Feeling/Result) | Chốt bằng cảm xúc / kết quả | *It helps me unwind. / I always feel refreshed afterwards.* |

#### 3. 7 Dạng câu hỏi Part 1 & Khung trả lời mẫu (7 Question Types)

##### 🔹 Type 1: Do you like...? / What's your favourite...?
- **Công thức**: `Answer` $\rightarrow$ `Reason` $\rightarrow$ `Example` $\rightarrow$ `Feeling`
- **Starters**: *Yes, I really do. / My favourite... is... / I'm really into...*
- **Sample**:
  > **Q:** *Do you like listening to music?*  
  > **A:** Yes, I really do. I like listening to music **because** it helps me relax after a long day. **For example**, I usually put on some soft songs in the evening when I have free time. **As a result**, I can clear my mind and forget all my daily worries.

##### 🔹 Type 2: How often...? / When...? / Where...?
- **Công thức**: `Answer` $\rightarrow$ `Details` $\rightarrow$ `Reason` $\rightarrow$ `Feeling/Result`
- **Starters**: *I usually... / I tend to... / I normally... / I often...*
- **Sample**:
  > **Q:** *How often do you exercise?*  
  > **A:** I usually exercise three or four times a week, mostly in the evening. I often go to the gym or ride my bike **because** I want to stay healthy and reduce stress after work. I always feel more energetic and refreshed **afterwards**.

##### 🔹 Type 3: What kind of...? / What type of...?
- **Công thức**: `Type` $\rightarrow$ `Reason` $\rightarrow$ `Example` $\rightarrow$ `Feeling`
- **Starters**: *I usually go for... / I like... / My favourite type of... is... / I'm really into...*
- **Sample**:
  > **Q:** *What kind of music do you like?*  
  > **A:** I usually go for pop music **because** it is catchy and easy to listen to. **For example**, I often listen to pop songs when I am travelling or relaxing at home. They help me unwind and instantly put me in a better mood.

##### 🔹 Type 4: Why...? / Why is... important?
- **Công thức**: `Answer` $\rightarrow$ `Reason 1` $\rightarrow$ `Reason 2 / Example` $\rightarrow$ `Feeling/Result`
- **Starters**: *I am keen on... because... / I think... is important because... / There are several reasons why...*
- **Sample**:
  > **Q:** *Why is learning English important?*  
  > **A:** I think learning English is important **for two main reasons**. **First**, it allows us to communicate with people from different countries. **Second**, it opens up better career and study opportunities in the future. **As a result**, I feel much more confident when using English.

##### 🔹 Type 5: What did you do...? / What were you doing when...?
- **Công thức (Past Event)**: `Past Event` $\rightarrow$ `Reason` $\rightarrow$ `Details` $\rightarrow$ `Feeling/Reflection`
- **Công thức (Interrupted Action)**: `I was + V-ing when + Past Simple`
- **Starters**: *The last time I... was... / I remember... / When I was..., I...*
- **Sample**:
  > **Q:** *What were you doing when you received some good news?*  
  > **A:** I remember studying at home **when** my friend gave me some good news. I **was preparing** for an important exam, so I felt quite stressed. I stopped studying immediately and called my friend to celebrate. I felt really excited and relieved.

##### 🔹 Type 6: Who...? / Who do you usually...?
- **Công thức**: `Person` $\rightarrow$ `Relationship` $\rightarrow$ `Activity` $\rightarrow$ `Reason/Feeling`
- **Lưu ý**: **Không bao giờ chỉ nêu tên người**. Luôn kết hợp Mối quan hệ + Hoạt động chung + Cảm xúc.
- **Starters**: *I usually spend time with... / I prefer... because... / My favourite person to... is...*
- **Sample**:
  > **Q:** *Who do you usually spend your free time with?*  
  > **A:** I usually spend my free time with my close friends. We often go to a coffee shop or have dinner together at the weekend. I enjoy spending time with them **because** we can share stories about our daily lives. It always helps me de-stress after a busy week.

##### 🔹 Type 7: What do you think about...? / Do you think...?
- **Công thức**: `Opinion / Direct Answer` $\rightarrow$ `Reason` $\rightarrow$ `Example` $\rightarrow$ `Result`
- **Starters**: *In my opinion,... / Personally, I believe that... / Yes, definitely. / Not really.*
- **Sample**:
  > **Q:** *Do you think young people should exercise regularly?*  
  > **A:** **Yes, definitely.** I think young people should exercise regularly **because** it keeps them fit and reduces mental stress. **For example**, they can go jogging, play sports, or take a walk after school or work. **As a result**, they will feel more energetic every day.

#### 6. AI Master Template - Speaking Part 1
> [!NOTE]
> Template dùng để AI tự động cấu trúc phản hồi bài nói Part 1.

```text
Let's talk about [Topic].
- First of all, regarding [Rephrase Question 1], [Direct Answer 1] because [Reason 1]. For example, [Example 1]. As a result, [Feeling/Result 1].
- Second of all, as for [Rephrase Question 2], [Direct Answer 2] because [Reason 2]. For example, [Example 2]. Therefore, [Feeling/Result 2].
- Third of all, concerning [Rephrase Question 3], [Direct Answer 3] because [Reason 3]. For instance, [Example 3]. It makes me feel [Feeling/Result 3].
```

### 📌 Part 2: Solution Discussion (4 Minutes)

#### 1. Cấu trúc bài nói Part 2
1. **Mở bài (Introduction)**: Nhắc lại tình huống + nêu 3 lựa chọn.
2. **Lựa chọn chính (Main Choice)**: Chọn 1 phương án + nêu 2 lý do ủng hộ.
3. **Bác bỏ (Rejection)**: Bác bỏ phương án 2 + nêu lý do. Bác bỏ phương án 3 + nêu lý do.
4. **Kết luận (Conclusion)**: Khẳng định lại lựa chọn tốt nhất.

#### 2. AI Master Template - Speaking Part 2

```text
[Rephrase Situation from Prompt]. 
There are three choices available: [Option A], [Option B], and [Option C]. 
In my opinion, I will choose [Selected Option] for several reasons.

First of all, it is [Reason 1 / Advantage 1]. For example, [Explanation/Detail 1].
Second of all, it [Reason 2 / Advantage 2]. This helps [Explanation/Detail 2].

On the other hand, I don't choose [Rejected Option 1] because it is [Disadvantage 1].
Similarly, I don't prefer [Rejected Option 2] because it is [Disadvantage 2].

To sum up, I strongly believe that [Selected Option] is the best choice for this situation.
```

#### 3. Bài mẫu hoàn chỉnh (Sample Part 2)
- **Situation**: One of your friends is planning a holiday with his friends. He asked you for advice about where to go: Sapa, Nha Trang, or Singapore.

```text
One of my friends is planning a holiday with his friends and he asked me for advice about where to go: Sapa, Nha Trang, or Singapore. 
There are three choices available: going to Sapa, Nha Trang, or Singapore. 
In my opinion, I will choose Nha Trang for several reasons. 

First of all, it is extremely beautiful with many famous beaches and delicious seafood. 
Second of all, traveling to Nha Trang is quite cheap and affordable for students.

On the other hand, I don't choose Sapa because it is very far away and tiring to travel by coach. 
Similarly, I don't prefer Singapore because it is an international trip, so it is very expensive and requires complex paperwork.

To sum up, I strongly believe that visiting Nha Trang is the best choice for them.
```

### 📌 Part 3: Topic Development (5 Minutes)

#### 1. Cấu trúc bài nói Part 3
1. **Mở bài**: Nêu chủ đề chính.
2. **Phát triển ý**:
   - **Ý 1**: Nêu nhánh 1 + giải thích/ví dụ.
   - **Ý 2**: Nêu nhánh 2 + giải thích/ví dụ.
   - **Ý 3**: Nêu nhánh 3 + giải thích/ví dụ.
3. **Kết luận**: Tóm tắt lại chủ đề.
4. **Trả lời câu hỏi phụ (Follow-up Questions)**: Trả lời 2–3 câu hỏi mở rộng từ giám khảo.

#### 2. AI Master Template - Speaking Part 3

```text
Well, I would like to talk about the topic: "[Main Topic Statement]".

- First of all, the first point I want to mention is [Point 1]. This is because [Explanation 1]. For instance, [Example 1].
- Second of all, the second point is [Point 2]. It means that [Explanation 2]. As a result, [Effect/Benefit 2].
- Third of all, the final point is [Point 3]. The main reason is that [Explanation 3].

To sum up, [Rephrase Main Topic Statement].
```

#### 3. Bài mẫu hoàn chỉnh (Sample Part 3)
- **Topic**: Smoking should be banned in public places (Smoker's health, Second-hand smoker's health, Financial burden).

```text
Well, I would like to talk about the topic: "Smoking should be banned in public places."

- First of all, the first point I want to mention is smoker's health. Smoking causes severe diseases such as lung cancer and heart problems, making it extremely dangerous for smokers themselves.
- Second of all, the second point is second-hand smoker's health. Non-smokers who inhale passive smoke can suffer from respiratory illnesses as well, especially children and pregnant women.
- Third of all, the final point is financial burden. Buying cigarettes regularly costs a lot of money that could otherwise be spent on education or healthier activities.

To sum up, I strongly agree that smoking should be banned in public places.
```

### ✉️ Part 1: Letter/Email Writing (120+ Words - 20 Minutes)

#### 1. Quy tắc làm bài
- Viết đủ **ít nhất 120 từ**.
- Phải trả lời **đầy đủ 3–4 câu hỏi** được yêu cầu trong đề.
- Mỗi câu hỏi nên triển khai thành 1 đoạn ngắn riêng biệt.

#### 2. AI Master Template - Informal / Semi-Formal Email

```text
Dear [Recipient Name],

I'm so happy to receive your email. How are your family and your work? Please send my warm regards to your parents.
I am writing this email to answer the questions you asked in your last email.

First of all, regarding [Question 1 Topic], [Answer 1 + Supporting Detail/Explanation].
Second of all, as for [Question 2 Topic], [Answer 2 + Supporting Detail/Explanation].
Third of all, concerning [Question 3 Topic], [Answer 3 + Supporting Detail/Explanation].
Finally, about [Question 4 Topic (if any)], [Answer 4 + Supporting Detail/Explanation].

I hope this information helps!
Looking forward to seeing you soon.

Best wishes,
[Sender Name]
```

#### 3. Bài mẫu hoàn chỉnh (Sample Writing Part 1)
- **Prompt**: An English friend (Kate) asks about your friend An, who wants to stay with her family in London.

```text
Dear Kate,

I'm so happy to receive your email. How are your family and your work? Please send my warm regards to your parents.
I am writing this email to answer the questions you asked in your last email about my friend, An.

First of all, speaking of what she is like, An is extremely intelligent, friendly, and hard-working. She is currently one of the top students in my class at university.

Second of all, regarding her hobbies and interests, she loves reading books and cooking. She frequently cooks delicious Vietnamese meals for her family, so I am sure she will prepare great dishes for your family too.

Finally, concerning her current study, she is studying Economics at Hanoi University. She is very responsible and polite, so I am confident that she will fit in with your family perfectly.

I hope this information helps!
Looking forward to seeing you soon.

Best wishes,
Lan
```

### 📝 Part 2: Essay Writing (250+ Words - 40 Minutes)

#### 🌐 Dạng 1: Advantages & Disadvantages Essay

##### AI Master Template:
```text
[Paraphrase Topic Context]. In recent years, this issue has become a major concern among the public. In this essay, I will discuss both the advantages and disadvantages of [Topic].

On the one hand, there are several distinct benefits associated with [Topic]. First of all, [Advantage 1 + Explanation/Example]. Second of all, [Advantage 2 + Explanation/Example].

On the other hand, certain drawbacks cannot be ignored. First of all, [Disadvantage 1 + Explanation/Example]. Second of all, [Disadvantage 2 + Explanation/Example].

To sum up, every coin has two sides. While there are some negative aspects, the positive impacts of [Topic] are also significant. In my personal opinion, I believe that [State Personal View].
```

---

#### ⚖️ Dạng 2: Agree or Disagree Essay

##### AI Master Template:
```text
[Paraphrase Topic Context]. This statement has sparked a lot of debate in society. In this essay, I will share my opinions on whether [Rephrase Target Statement].

On the one hand, there are valid reasons to support this view. First of all, [Supporting Argument 1 + Explanation/Example]. Second of all, [Supporting Argument 2 + Explanation/Example].

On the other hand, opponents of this idea present strong counterarguments. First of all, [Opposing Argument 1 + Explanation/Example]. Second of all, [Opposing Argument 2 + Explanation/Example].

In conclusion, every issue has two sides. After examining both perspectives, I strongly [Agree / Disagree] with the idea that [Rephrase Target Statement].
```

## 🗂️ C. PRACTICE QUESTION BANK (NGÂN HÀNG ĐỀ THI THỰC HÀNH)

> [!TIP]
> Dùng danh sách đề bài dưới đây làm Prompt đầu vào cho AI để yêu cầu sinh bài mẫu chuẩn VSTEP.

### 🗣️ Speaking Practice Sets

#### Set 1: Social Interaction (Part 1)
1. **Games and Sports**: Which indoor games do you play regularly? | Which indoor games are common in your hometown? | Do you take part in any outdoor sports regularly?
2. **Keeping Fit**: How often do you do any exercise? | Do you prefer to exercise alone or in a class? Why? | What do you think is the best type of exercise? Why?
3. **Shopping**: Where do you usually go shopping? | Who do you like going shopping with? | Do you prefer going shopping alone or with friends?

5. **House & Home**: How many rooms are there in your house? | What is your favorite room in the house? | What do you want to change in your house?
6. **Neighborhood**: Can you tell me something about your neighborhood? | What do you like most about it? | Do you plan to live there for a long time? Why/why not?

#### Set 2: Solution Discussion (Part 2)

3. **Pet Selection**: Giving a pet to grandparents in the countryside: a dog, a cat, or a fish. Which is most suitable?
4. **Lottery Spending**: A friend won 1,000,000,000 VNĐ. Options: buy a house, buy a car, or save in the bank. Choose the best idea.

3. **Environmental Protection**:
   - *Main Idea*: There are some ways to protect the environment.
   - *Gợi ý*: Use alternative energy | Use public transport | Recycle | Your own idea.
   - *Follow-up Questions*: What can you recycle? | Have you done enough to protect the environment? | Should schools teach environmental protection?
