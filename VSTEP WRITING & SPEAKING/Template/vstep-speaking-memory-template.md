# 🗣️ VSTEP Speaking: Memory Template

> Use this as a **memory sheet** and as a **prompt reference** for AI: "Write my answer using this template."

---

## 1. Test structure

| Part | Time | Task | Your job |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** Social Interaction | 3 min | 2 topics × 3 questions | 3-5 sentences per question (20-30 s) |
| **2** Solution Discussion | 4 min | 1 situation, choose 1 solution | Pick 1, reject the others |
| **3** Topic Development | 5 min | 1 topic + 3 ideas, then follow-up questions | 3 points + conclusion |

---

## 2. Part 1: A.R.E.F

**Answer → Reason → Example → Feeling**

```text
Let's talk about [Topic].
1. Regarding [Q1], [Answer] because [Reason]. For example, [Example]. I feel [Feeling].
2. As for [Q2], [Answer] because [Reason]. For instance, [Example]. As a result, [Result].
3. Concerning [Q3], [Answer] because [Reason]. For example, [Example]. It makes me feel [Feeling].
```

### Question-word shortcut

| Question | Start with |
|---|---|
| Do you…? / Can you…? | Yes, I really do. / Not really. |
| How often / When / Where | I usually… / I tend to… / I normally… |
| What kind of… | I usually go for… / I'm really into… |
| Why…? / Why is… important? | I think… because… / There are two reasons… |
| Who…? | Never just a name: **Person + Relationship + Activity** |
| Past (What did you do…?) | The last time I… was… |
| Interrupted past | `I was + V-ing when + past simple` |
| What do you think…? | In my opinion… / Personally, I believe… |

### Sample test: Games and Sports

| Question | Model answer |
|---|---|
| Which indoor games do you play regularly? | I usually play chess and card games **because** they are fun and make me think. **For example**, I play chess with my friends at the weekend. I always feel relaxed **afterwards**. |
| Which indoor games are common in your hometown? | Board games and card games are very common **because** they are cheap and easy to play. **For instance**, many families play cards together during holidays. **As a result**, they feel closer to each other. |
| Do you take part in any outdoor sports regularly? | Yes, I play badminton twice a week **because** it keeps me fit and reduces stress. **For example**, I usually play with my colleagues after work. I always feel energetic **afterwards**. |

### Sample test: Keeping Fit

| Question | Model answer |
|---|---|
| How often do you do any exercise? | I usually exercise three times a week, mostly in the evening. I go jogging or to the gym **because** I want to stay healthy. I feel more refreshed **after** that. |
| Alone or in a class? Why? | I prefer exercising alone **because** I can choose my own time and pace. **For example**, I can go jogging whenever I'm free. **As a result**, I feel comfortable and relaxed. |
| Best type of exercise? Why? | I think the best type is swimming **because** it works the whole body and is gentle on the joints. **For example**, swimming 30 minutes helps me burn calories and relax. It makes me feel **refreshed**. |

## 3. Part 2: Choose 1, reject the others

```text
[Rephrase situation]. There are two/three choices: [A], [B] (and [C]).
In my opinion, I will choose [A] for several reasons.

First of all, it is [Reason 1]. For example, [Detail].
Second of all, it [Reason 2]. This helps [Detail].

On the other hand, I don't choose [B] because it is [Disadvantage].
Similarly, I don't prefer [C] because it is [Disadvantage].

To sum up, I strongly believe that [A] is the best choice.
```

### Sample: foreign English centre or Vietnamese centre?

> We are discussing whether to learn English in a foreign English centre or in a Vietnamese centre. In my opinion, I will choose a **foreign English centre** for several reasons.
>
> First of all, the teachers are native speakers, so students learn **correct pronunciation**. For example, they can hear natural accents every day.
> Second of all, students have more chances to **speak and communicate** in English. This helps them become more confident.
>
> On the other hand, I don't choose a Vietnamese centre **because** teachers often explain in Vietnamese, so students have fewer chances to practise speaking. In addition, they focus more on grammar and tests than on real communication.
>
> To sum up, I strongly believe that a foreign English centre is the best choice.

> [!TIP]
> With only 2 options, reject 1. To sound more B2, add a concession: *"Although Vietnamese centres are cheaper, ..."*

## 4. Part 3: 3 points + conclusion

```text
Well, I would like to talk about the topic: "[Topic]".

First of all, the first point I want to mention is [Point 1]. This is because [Explanation]. For instance, [Example].
Second of all, the second point is [Point 2]. It means that [Explanation]. As a result, [Effect].
Third of all, the final point is [Point 3]. The main reason is that [Explanation].

To sum up, [Rephrase topic + feeling].
```

### Sample: Describe a person who has a strong influence on you

Points: **Who they are** → **What they are like** → **How they changed me**

> Well, I would like to talk about a person who has a strong influence on me: **my mother**.
>
> First of all, the first point I want to mention is **who she is**. She is a hard-working woman who has taken care of our family for many years.
> Second of all, the second point is **her personality**. She is patient, kind and always honest. For instance, she never gives up when facing difficulties.
> Third of all, the final point is **how she influences me**. She taught me to work hard and be responsible. As a result, I have become more independent and confident.
>
> To sum up, my mother is the most important person in my life, and I am truly grateful to her.

### Follow-up questions (3-4 sentences)

**Opinion → Reason → Example.** Start with: *In my opinion… / Yes, definitely… / Not really…*

## 5. Connectors bank (conjunctions)

### 5.1 By function

| Function             | Words                                                                                                                       | Use in a sentence                                              |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Reason**           | because · since · as · the main reason is that · that's why · due to                                                        | I exercise **because** it reduces stress.                      |
| **Example**          | for example · for instance · such as · like · especially · in particular                                                    | I enjoy sports **such as** badminton.                          |
| **Add**              | and · also · besides · in addition · apart from that · moreover · what's more · not only… but also                          | **Besides**, it saves me a lot of time.                        |
| **Contrast**         | but · however · although · even though · while · on the other hand · whereas                                                | **Although** I'm busy, I still exercise.                       |
| **Result**           | so · as a result · therefore · consequently · that's why · thanks to this                                                   | **As a result**, I feel more confident.                        |
| **Order**            | first · second · third · firstly · after that · then · next · afterwards · finally                                          | **First**, it helps me relax. **Finally**, I go home and rest. |
| **Time**             | when · while · before · after · as soon as · during · until · whenever                                                      | I was studying **when** my friend called.                      |
| **Condition**        | if · unless · as long as · otherwise                                                                                        | **If** I have free time, I go jogging.                         |
| **Purpose**          | to · in order to · so that                                                                                                  | I go to the gym **in order to** stay fit.                      |
| **Opinion**          | in my opinion · personally, I believe · from my point of view · I strongly believe · to be honest · as far as I'm concerned | **From my point of view**, it is necessary.                    |
| **Agree / Disagree** | Yes, definitely · Yes, I do · Absolutely · I totally agree · Not really · I don't think so · I partly agree                 | **Not really.** I don't think it's entirely good.              |
| **Compare**          | similarly · likewise · in the same way · compared with · more… than                                                         | **Similarly**, I don't prefer Singapore.                       |
| **Emphasis**         | in fact · actually · indeed · especially · above all                                                                        | **Above all**, it's good for my health.                        |
| **Conclusion**       | to sum up · in conclusion · all in all · overall · in short                                                                 | **To sum up**, I strongly believe that…                        |
| **Fillers**          | Well, let me think… · That's an interesting question. · Let me see… · To be honest… · Actually…                             | **Well, let me think…** I usually go jogging.                  |

### 5.2 Which connectors for which part

| Part | Core connectors to use |
|---|---|
| **Part 1** | because · for example · as a result · especially · and · so · after that |
| **Part 2** | In my opinion · for several reasons · First of all / Second of all · On the other hand · Similarly · Although · To sum up |
| **Part 3** | First of all / Second of all / Third of all · This is because · For instance · As a result · The main reason is that · To sum up |

### 5.3 Smooth chain (build a long sentence step by step)

```text
Short:    I play badminton.
+ Reason: I play badminton because it keeps me fit.
+ Detail: I play badminton twice a week because it keeps me fit and reduces stress.
+ Example: ...For example, I usually play with my colleagues after work.
+ Result: ...As a result, I always feel energetic afterwards.
```

**Joining two ideas fast:** `idea 1 + , and / but / so + idea 2` → *It is cheap, **so** many families play it.*

**Linking sentences:** end sentence A → start sentence B with `Besides, / However, / As a result, / For instance,`

## 5C. Emotion + connector in one answer (full model)

**Q: Do you take part in any outdoor sports regularly?**

> **Yes, I do.** I play badminton twice a week **because** it keeps me fit **and** reduces stress. **For example**, I usually play with my colleagues after work, **especially** at the weekend. **As a result**, I always feel **energetic and refreshed** afterwards.

| Piece | Words used |
|---|---|
| Answer | Yes, I do |
| Reason connector | because · and |
| Example connector | For example · especially |
| Result connector | As a result |
| Feeling | energetic and refreshed |

## 7. Tense checklist

| Situation | Tense | Correct |
|---|---|---|
| Habit | Present simple | I usually **go** |
| Finished past | Past simple | I **went**, I **met** |
| Interrupted action | Past continuous + past simple | I **was studying** when she **called** |
| He/She/It | add -s | She **gives** advice |
| Singular noun | article | **a** coffee shop |
| Plural noun | -s | many **friends** |

## 8. One-page cheat sheet (memorize this)

| | Skeleton | Key phrases |
|---|---|---|
| **Part 1** | **A**nswer → **R**eason → **E**xample → **F**eeling | because · for example · as a result · I feel… |
| **Part 2** | Rephrase → Choose → 2 reasons → Reject → Sum up | I will choose… for several reasons · On the other hand, I don't choose… · Similarly… · To sum up… |
| **Part 3** | Topic → Point 1 → Point 2 → Point 3 → Sum up | First of all… Second of all… Third of all… · To sum up… |

### Memory quotes

- **"Never one word."** At least Answer + Reason + Feeling.
- **"Answer first, reason next."**
- **"Choose one, reject the rest, then sum up."**
- **"Three points, three examples, one conclusion."**
- **"Fluency beats fancy words."** Self-correct with *Sorry, I mean…*, never go silent.

## 9. AI prompt (copy and paste)

```text
Use my VSTEP Speaking template. Write a model answer for this question:
[PASTE QUESTION]
Part: [1 / 2 / 3]
Level: B2, natural spoken English, 20-30 seconds per answer (Part 1).
Follow: A.R.E.F (Part 1) / Choose-1-reject-others (Part 2) / 3 points + conclusion (Part 3).
Use connectors: because, for example, as a result, first of all, to sum up.
```

## 10. 7-day routine (15 min/day)

| Day | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1 | Part 1, Types 1-2: read samples aloud 3 times |
| 2 | Part 1, Types 3-4: cover the sample, rebuild from the formula |
| 3 | Part 1, Types 5-6: past tense and "who" answers |
| 4 | Part 2: practise choose + reject with 3 new situations |
| 5 | Part 3: 3 points + conclusion with 2 new topics |
| 6 | Mix all parts, record yourself, fix tenses and missing steps |
| 7 | Mock test: Part 1 + 2 + 3, no notes |
