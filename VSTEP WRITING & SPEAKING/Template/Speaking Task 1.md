## TYPE 1: DO YOU LIKE...? / WHAT'S YOUR FAVOURITE...?

> **Overview:** Common question types in VSTEP Speaking Part 1[span_0](start_span)[span_0](end_span). Keep answers simple and follow a structured sequence[span_1](start_span)[span_1](end_span):
> **Answer → Reason → Example → Feeling**[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span)

### Speaking Framework
* **Answer:** Yes, I really do. / My favourite... is...[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span)
* **Reason:** ...because...[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span)
* **Example:** For example, I usually...[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span)
* **Feeling:** I feel... / It helps me...[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span)

### Sample Questions & Model Answers
1. **Do you like listening to music?**[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span)
   * Yes, I really do. I like listening to music because it helps me relax after a long day. I usually listen to music when I have some free time, especially in the evening. It helps me clear my mind and forget about my daily worries.[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span)
2. **What's your favourite sport?**[span_9](start_span)[span_9](end_span)
   * My favourite sport is badminton because it is both fun and good for my health. I usually play it with my friends at the weekend. It helps me stay active and gives me a chance to spend time with people I enjoy being with. I always feel refreshed after playing.[span_10](start_span)[span_10](end_span)
3. **Do you like reading books?**[span_11](start_span)[span_11](end_span)
   * Yes, I really do. I enjoy reading books because I can learn new things and expand my knowledge. I usually read books about science or personal development before going to bed. It is a great way for me to relax and learn something useful at the same time.[span_12](start_span)[span_12](end_span)
4. **What's your favourite season?**[span_13](start_span)[span_13](end_span)
   * My favourite season is winter because I really enjoy the cool and comfortable weather. I usually spend more time outdoors during this season, especially going for walks or having coffee with my friends. I don't have to worry about the heat, so I feel more comfortable and relaxed.[span_14](start_span)[span_14](end_span)
5. **Do you like travelling?**[span_15](start_span)[span_15](end_span)
   * Yes, I really do. I like travelling because it allows me to take a break from my daily routine and discover new places. For example, I really enjoy visiting beaches because I love watching the sunset and enjoying the fresh air. After a trip, I usually feel more relaxed and recharged.[span_16](start_span)[span_16](end_span)

## TYPE 2: HOW OFTEN...? / WHEN...? / WHERE...?

> **Overview:** Universal formula for questions about habits and daily routines[span_17](start_span)[span_17](end_span):
> **Answer → Details → Reason → Feeling/Result**[span_18](start_span)[span_18](end_span)

### Opening Starters
* I usually...[span_19](start_span)[span_19](end_span)
* I tend to...[span_20](start_span)[span_20](end_span)
* I normally...[span_21](start_span)[span_21](end_span)
* I often...[span_22](start_span)[span_22](end_span)

### Quick Memory Formula
* **Answer:** I usually... / I tend to...[span_23](start_span)[span_23](end_span)
* **Details:** when / where / how often...[span_24](start_span)[span_24](end_span)
* **Reason:** ...because...[span_25](start_span)[span_25](end_span)

### Sample Questions & Model Answers
1. **How often do you exercise?**[span_27](start_span)[span_27](end_span)

2. **When do you usually listen to music?**[span_29](start_span)[span_29](end_span)
   * I tend to listen to music in the evening, especially when I have some free time. I usually listen to my favourite songs while relaxing at home because music helps me unwind after a busy day. It always puts me in a better mood.[span_30](start_span)[span_30](end_span)
3. **Where do you usually study?**[span_31](start_span)[span_31](end_span)
   * I usually study at home because it is quiet and comfortable. I normally sit at my desk and study in the evening, especially when I have an important exam. I can concentrate better there, so I feel more productive.[span_32](start_span)[span_32](end_span)
4. **When do you usually meet your friends?**[span_33](start_span)[span_33](end_span)
   * I normally meet my friends at the weekend because we are all quite busy during the week. We often go to a coffee shop or have dinner together and talk about our daily lives. I always feel happy and relaxed after spending time with them.[span_34](start_span)[span_34](end_span)
5. **Where do you usually go in your free time?**[span_35](start_span)[span_35](end_span)
   * I often go to a coffee shop near my home when I have some free time. I usually meet my friends there or read a book because it is a peaceful place to relax. I always feel refreshed after spending some time there.[span_36](start_span)[span_36](end_span)

## TYPE 5: WHAT DID YOU DO...? / WHAT WERE YOU DOING WHEN...?

> **Overview:** General framework for past events and memories[span_77](start_span)[span_77](end_span):
> **Past Event → Reason → Details → Feeling/Reflection**[span_78](start_span)[span_78](end_span)

### Opening Starters
* The last time I... was...[span_79](start_span)[span_79](end_span)

* When I was..., I...[span_81](start_span)[span_81](end_span)

### Past Activity Template
* **Past Event:** The last time I [did something] was [time].[span_83](start_span)[span_83](end_span)
* **Reason:** I [activity] because [reason].[span_84](start_span)[span_84](end_span)
* **Details:** I [give 1–2 details about the activity].[span_85](start_span)[span_85](end_span)
* **Feeling/Reflection:** I felt [feeling] because [reflection/result].[span_86](start_span)[span_86](end_span)

### Interrupting Past Event Template
*(For questions using "What were you doing when...?")*[span_87](start_span)[span_87](end_span)
* **Grammar Structure:** `I was + V-ing when + Past Simple`[span_88](start_span)[span_88](end_span)
* **Activity:** I was [activity] when [something happened].[span_89](start_span)[span_89](end_span)
* **Follow-up:** I [describe what I did next].[span_90](start_span)[span_90](end_span)
* **Feeling:** I felt [feeling] because [reason/reflection].[span_91](start_span)[span_91](end_span)

### Sample Questions & Model Answers

3. **What did you do the last time you met your best friend?**[span_96](start_span)[span_96](end_span)

> **Person → Relationship → Activity → Reason/Feeling**[span_99](start_span)[span_99](end_span)

### Opening Starters
