# VSTEP Speaking Practice Templates (Part 1)

---

## TYPE 1: DO YOU LIKE...? / WHAT'S YOUR FAVOURITE...?

> **Overview:** Common question types in VSTEP Speaking Part 1. Keep answers simple and follow a structured sequence:
> **Answer → Reason → Example → Feeling**

### Speaking Framework
* **Answer:** Yes, I really do. / My favourite... is...
* **Reason:** ...because...
* **Example:** For example, I usually...
* **Feeling:** I feel... / It helps me...

### Sample Questions & Model Answers
1. **Do you like listening to music?**
   * Yes, I really do. I like listening to music because it helps me relax after a long day. I usually listen to music when I have some free time, especially in the evening. It helps me clear my mind and forget about my daily worries.
2. **What's your favourite sport?**
   * My favourite sport is badminton because it is both fun and good for my health. I usually play it with my friends at the weekend. It helps me stay active and gives me a chance to spend time with people I enjoy being with. I always feel refreshed after playing.
3. **Do you like reading books?**
   * Yes, I really do. I enjoy reading books because I can learn new things and expand my knowledge. I usually read books about science or personal development before going to bed. It is a great way for me to relax and learn something useful at the same time.
4. **What's your favourite season?**
   * My favourite season is winter because I really enjoy the cool and comfortable weather. I usually spend more time outdoors during this season, especially going for walks or having coffee with my friends. I don't have to worry about the heat, so I feel more comfortable and relaxed.
5. **Do you like travelling?**
   * Yes, I really do. I like travelling because it allows me to take a break from my daily routine and discover new places. For example, I really enjoy visiting beaches because I love watching the sunset and enjoying the fresh air. After a trip, I usually feel more relaxed and recharged.

## TYPE 2: HOW OFTEN...? / WHEN...? / WHERE...?

> **Overview:** Universal formula for questions about habits and daily routines:
> **Answer → Details → Reason → Feeling/Result**

### Opening Starters
* I usually...
* I tend to...
* I normally...
* I often...

### Quick Memory Formula
* **Answer:** I usually... / I tend to...
* **Details:** when / where / how often...
* **Reason:** ...because...
* **Feeling/Result:** I feel... / It helps me...

### Sample Questions & Model Answers
1. **How often do you exercise?**
   * I usually exercise three or four times a week, mostly in the evening. I often go to the gym or take a bike ride after work because I want to stay healthy and reduce stress. I always feel more energetic and relaxed afterwards.
2. **When do you usually listen to music?**
   * I tend to listen to music in the evening, especially when I have some free time. I usually listen to my favourite songs while relaxing at home because music helps me unwind after a busy day. It always puts me in a better mood.
3. **Where do you usually study?**
   * I usually study at home because it is quiet and comfortable. I normally sit at my desk and study in the evening, especially when I have an important exam. I can concentrate better there, so I feel more productive.
4. **When do you usually meet your friends?**
   * I normally meet my friends at the weekend because we are all quite busy during the week. We often go to a coffee shop or have dinner together and talk about our daily lives. I always feel happy and relaxed after spending time with them.
5. **Where do you usually go in your free time?**
   * I often go to a coffee shop near my home when I have some free time. I usually meet my friends there or read a book because it is a peaceful place to relax. I always feel refreshed after spending some time there.

### Quick Memory Formula

## TYPE 4: WHY...? / WHY IS ... IMPORTANT?

> **Overview:** Structure for giving reasons and explaining importance:
> **Answer → Reason 1 → Reason 2/Example → Feeling/Result**

### Opening Starters
* I am keen on... because...
* I think... is important because...
* There are several reasons why I...
* One of the main reasons is that...

### Quick Memory Formula
* **Answer:** I think... / I am keen on...
* **Reason:** ...because...
* **Example:** For example...
* **Result/Feeling:** As a result, I feel... / It helps me...

### Sample Questions & Model Answers
1. **Why do you like listening to music?**
   * I am keen on listening to music because it helps me relax after a long day. For example, I often listen to my favourite songs before going to bed or when I am travelling. It helps me clear my mind and puts me in a better mood.
2. **Why is exercise important?**
   * I think exercise is important because it helps us stay healthy and reduce stress. For example, people can go jogging, go to the gym, or simply take a walk in the evening. Regular exercise makes me feel more energetic and improves my overall well-being.
3. **Why is learning English important?**
   * I think learning English is important because it allows us to communicate with people from different countries. It can also give us more opportunities to study and work in the future. As a result, I feel more confident when using English in my daily life.
4. **Why do you like travelling?**
   * I am keen on travelling because it allows me to take a break from my daily routine and discover new places. For example, I really enjoy visiting beaches and trying local food when I travel. I always feel relaxed and refreshed after a trip.
5. **Why is spending time with family important?**
   * I think spending time with family is important because it helps us maintain close relationships and support each other. For example, my family usually has meals together or watches movies at the weekend. These activities make me feel happy and connected to my family.

## TYPE 5: WHAT DID YOU DO...? / WHAT WERE YOU DOING WHEN...?

> **Overview:** General framework for past events and memories:
> **Past Event → Reason → Details → Feeling/Reflection**

### Opening Starters
* The last time I... was...
* I remember...
* When I was..., I...
* Last..., I...

### Past Activity Template
* **Past Event:** The last time I [did something] was [time].
* **Reason:** I [activity] because [reason].
* **Details:** I [give 1–2 details about the activity].
* **Feeling/Reflection:** I felt [feeling] because [reflection/result].

### Interrupting Past Event Template
*(For questions using "What were you doing when...?")*
* **Grammar Structure:** `I was + V-ing when + Past Simple`
* **Activity:** I was [activity] when [something happened].
* **Follow-up:** I [describe what I did next].
* **Feeling:** I felt [feeling] because [reason/reflection].

### Sample Questions & Model Answers
1. **What did you do the last time you went shopping?**
   * The last time I went shopping was a few days ago. I went to a shopping mall because I needed to buy some new clothes. I bought a T-shirt and a pair of jeans, and I also had lunch there. I felt quite happy because I found everything I needed.
2. **What were you doing when you received some good news?**
   * I remember studying at home when I received some good news from my friend. I was preparing for an English test, so I was quite stressed at the time. I stopped studying for a while and called my friend to talk about it. I felt really excited and relieved.
3. **What did you do the last time you met your best friend?**
   * The last time I met my best friend was last week. We went to a small coffee shop because we wanted to catch up after a busy week. We talked about our work, watched some videos, and took a few photos together. I felt really relaxed because I always enjoy spending time with my friend.

## TYPE 6: WHO...? / WHO DO YOU USUALLY...?

> **Overview:** Avoid giving just a name and stopping. Always expand with **Relationship + Shared Activity + Reason/Feeling**.
> **Person → Relationship → Activity → Reason/Feeling**

### Opening Starters
* I usually spend time with...
* I prefer... because...
* I often go with...
* My favourite person to... is...

### Quick Memory Formula
* **Person:** I usually spend time with...
* **Relationship:** He / She is my...
* **Activity:** We often...
* **Reason/Feeling:** I enjoy it because... / It makes me feel...

### Sample Questions & Model Answers
1. **Who do you usually spend your free time with?**
   * I usually spend my free time with my close friends. We often go to coffee shops or have dinner together at the weekend. I enjoy spending time with them because we can talk about our daily lives and have fun together. It always helps me relax after a busy week.
2. **Who do you usually go shopping with?**
   * I usually go shopping with my sister because we have similar tastes. We often go to shopping centres at the weekend and look for clothes or other things we need. I enjoy shopping with her because she always gives me useful advice.
3. **Who is your favourite person to travel with?**
   * My favourite person to travel with is my best friend. We often take short trips together because we both enjoy exploring new places. For example, we like visiting beaches and trying local food. I always feel comfortable and excited when I travel with her.
4. **Who do you usually study with?**
   * I usually study with one of my close friends. We often meet at a quiet café or study together online when we have important exams. We can share ideas and help each other when we have difficulties. I find studying with a friend more enjoyable and motivating.
5. **Who do you usually talk to when you have a problem?**
   * I usually talk to my best friend when I have a problem. We have known each other for several years, so I feel comfortable sharing my thoughts with her. She always listens to me and gives me useful advice. Talking to her helps me feel calmer and more confident.

## TYPE 7: WHY...? / WHAT DO YOU THINK...? / DO YOU THINK...?

> **Opinion / Answer → Reason → Example → Result**

# VSTEP Speaking Part 1: Complete Study Guide

> Goal: 3-5 sentences, 20-30 seconds per answer. Never answer in one word.

---

## PART A: MASTER FORMULA

**A.R.E.F.** = **A**nswer → **R**eason → **E**xample → **F**eeling

| Step | Job | Starters |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | Answer directly | *Yes, I really do. / I tend to... / My favourite... is...* |
| **R** | Give a reason | *because... / since... / the main reason is that...* |
| **E** | Give an example | *For instance,... / For example,... / Last week I...* |
| **F** | Finish with feeling/result | *It helps me unwind. / I always feel refreshed.* |

---

## PART B: CONNECTORS (CONJUNCTIONS) BANK

### 1. Reason
| Word | Example |
|---|---|
| because | I like it **because** it helps me relax. |
| since / as | **Since** I'm busy, I only go at weekends. |
| the main reason is that | **The main reason is that** it's good for my health. |
| that's why | It's quiet, **that's why** I study there. |

### 2. Example
| Word | Example |
|---|---|
| for example / for instance | **For instance**, I often go jogging in the park. |
| such as | I enjoy outdoor sports **such as** badminton. |
| especially / in particular | I love the beach, **especially** at sunset. |

### 3. Adding ideas
| Word | Example |
|---|---|
| and / also | I like pho **and** spring rolls. |
| besides / in addition | **Besides**, it saves me a lot of time. |
| apart from that | **Apart from that**, it's very cheap. |
| not only... but also... | It's **not only** fun **but also** healthy. |

### 4. Contrast
| Word | Example |
|---|---|
| but / however | It's tiring, **but** I enjoy it. |
| although / even though | **Although** I'm busy, I still exercise. |
| on the other hand | **On the other hand**, it can be expensive. |

### 5. Result
| Word | Example |
|---|---|
| so / as a result | **As a result**, I feel more confident. |
| therefore / consequently | I sleep well, **therefore** I'm full of energy. |

### 6. Sequence
| Word | Example |
|---|---|
| first / firstly | **First**, it helps me relax. |
| second / secondly | **Second**, it improves my health. |
| after that / afterwards / then | **Afterwards**, I always feel refreshed. |
| finally | **Finally**, I go home and rest. |

### 7. Time
| Word | Example |
|---|---|
| when / while | I was studying **when** my friend called. |
| as soon as | **As soon as** I got home, I called her. |
| before / after | I read a book **before** going to bed. |

### 8. Opinion
| Word | Example |
|---|---|
| in my opinion | **In my opinion**, it's a good idea. |
| personally, I believe | **Personally, I believe** exercise is vital. |
| from my point of view | **From my point of view**, it's necessary. |
| to be honest | **To be honest**, I prefer staying at home. |

### 9. Fillers (when you need time to think)
- *Well, let me think...*
- *That's an interesting question.*
- *To be honest,...*
- *Let me see...*

## PART E: TENSE CHECKLIST

| Situation | Tense | Correct | Wrong |
|---|---|---|---|
| Habit | Present simple | I usually **go** | I usually going |
| Finished past | Past simple | I **went**, I **met** | I go yesterday |
| Interrupted action | Past continuous + past simple | I **was studying** when she **called** | I studied when she calling |
| He/She/It | add -s | She **gives** advice | She give advice |
| Singular noun | article | **a** coffee shop | coffee shop |
| Plural noun | -s | many **friends** | many friend |

---

## PART F: EXAM TIPS

1. **Never answer in one word.** Use at least A + R + F.
2. **Truth doesn't matter.** Say what you can express fluently.
3. **Keep answers short.** Part 1 has 2 topics × 3 questions.
4. **Self-correct calmly:** *Sorry, I mean...* is fine. Silence is worse.
5. **Steady pace.** Fluency beats complex words.

### Memory tricks

- **Say it aloud.** Speaking practice only works if you speak.
- **Shadowing:** read a sample, then repeat it without looking.
- **Swap method:** reuse one sample but change the topic (music → films).
- **3 new connectors per day.** Use them in real answers.
- **Record and compare** with the model answer.
