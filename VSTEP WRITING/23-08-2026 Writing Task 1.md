### 📋 Writing Task 1

> You have received an email from your **friend**, Alex. He is doing a survey about magazines and wants to know about your reading habits.
> 
> Write a letter to Alex. In your letter, answer these questions:
> 
> - What kind of magazines do you like?
> - How often do you buy them?
> - Do you prefer reading magazines online or on paper?


### ✍️ Template

> Dear my friend,  
> How are you ? I hope you and your family are fine. Many thanks for your last letter. It is great to hear from you again. I haven't written for such a long time, I have been really busy going to work. So now I am writing this letter to answer your questions/ respond to you.
> 
> Firstly, the kind of magazines I like are comedy and marvels because they are very fun and relaxing to read.
> 
> Secondly, I buy them once a week because they are quite cheap and easy to buy.
> 
> Finally, I prefer reading magazines online because it is convenient and fast. However, reading magazines on paper is also very fun and easy to bring along.
> 
> Well this is all from me now Alex. I am really looking forward to hearing from you. Remember to write to me whenever you have free time and let me know about what you want and think about my letter.
> 
> Yours sincerely,  
> Johnny Pham
