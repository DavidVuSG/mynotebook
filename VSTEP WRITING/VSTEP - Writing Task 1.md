You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.
Write about the following topic:
Some people think that working from home brings more benefits than working in an office, while others believe that it reduces productivity and teamwork.
 Which opinion do you agree with?
Use specific reasons and examples to support your answer.
You should write at least 250 words.
BÀI LÀM: 
In recent years, remote work has become a common trend because technology now allows people to work efficiently from anywhere. While some argue that it reduces teamwork and productivity, I strongly believe that working from home offers more benefits, especially in terms of flexibility and personal well-being.
One clear advantage of remote work is flexibility, since employees can manage their schedules according to their own routines. They do not need to spend hours commuting, so they can use that time to rest or take care of their families. For example, when I worked online during the pandemic, I had more time to exercise and prepare healthy meals, which improved both my mood and concentration. Although it requires self-discipline, working from home often helps people perform better because they can create a comfortable and distraction-free environment.
Another benefit is that remote work supports a better work-life balance. People can stay close to their loved ones and reduce daily stress, which leads to higher job satisfaction. Furthermore, with the help of online platforms like Zoom and Google Meet, communication and teamwork have become much easier than before. Even though colleagues are not physically together, they can still collaborate effectively and share ideas instantly.
Of course, remote work isn’t perfect, as some people may feel lonely or find it hard to separate personal life from work. However, these issues can be managed with good planning and regular virtual meetings.
In conclusion, although remote work has a few downsides, its flexibility, convenience, and positive impact on mental health make it an excellent option for many modern workers.
